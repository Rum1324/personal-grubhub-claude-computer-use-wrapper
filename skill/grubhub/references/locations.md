# Cal Dining meal-equivalency locations (Grubhub app)

Found via app search "Meal Equivalency" (UC Berkeley Dining filter). Every item costs **1M** (one meal equivalency). Menus are time-of-day based: outside open hours the app hides most/all items.

Last surveyed: 2026-09-22 ~10pm (after close — lunch mains not visible; re-survey during open hours).

**"Pickup hours" = Grubhub mobile-order window, not the café's door hours.** Golden Bear Café stays open in person Mon–Fri 7:30am–7pm (dining.berkeley.edu, AY 2026/27), but app pickup orders stop at 5:30pm. An app order placed before 5:30 can be collected later while the café is still open.

## The Golden Bear Café — south, closest
App listing "The Golden Bear Café" → "Choose a menu": GBC - Full Menu / GBC - Meal Equivalency.
App-listed cuisines: breakfast sandwiches, burritos, Philly cheesesteak, salads, fries, fruit salad, lunch meals.
Known item: **Cheeseburger** — Side (req): French Fries · Side Green Salad · No Side · Fruit Salad; Beverage (req): Milk · Orange Juice · Hot Tea · Coffee · Proud Source · Diet Pepsi · Pepsi · Gatorade ×3 · No Beverage; Customize (optional, up to 7). Also Buffalo Chicken Fries.

## Ladle & Leaf — south (MLK)
All come with a beverage.
- **½ Sandwich + ½ Soup:** ½ Vegan Chicken Salad · ½ Turkey Pesto (turkey, pesto, romaine, tomato, mayo on roll)
- **½ Salad + ½ Soup:** ½ Asian Chicken or Tofu · ½ Cabo Grilled Chicken · ½ Chipotle Chicken
- **Full Salads:** Asian Chicken or Tofu · Cabo Grilled Chicken · Chipotle Chicken
- **Grain Bowls:** Quinoa Feta Avocado · Pico Kale
- Soup choices: Minestrone · Hot & Sour Chicken · Organic Tomato Bisque
- Drink choices: Proud Source · Diet Pepsi · Gatorade (Orange / Lemon Lime / Fruit Punch) · No Drink

## Monsoon Kitchens — south (MLK, Indian)
One item: **Katora Bowl + Beverage** (build your own)
- Base (req): Basmati Rice · Ancient Grain Blend
- Protein/curry (req): Chicken Tikka Masala · Palak Paneer · Cali Coconut Curry · No Protein
- Side (req): Alu Chole · Tarka Dal · No Side
- Drink (req): Proud Source · Diet Pepsi Can · Pepsi Can · Gatorade (3 flavors) · No Drink
- Top it Off (optional, up to 5): Tamarind Chutney · Tomato Chutney · Cilantro Chutney · Kachumber · No Sauce

## Gateway Cafe — north side
Evening menu only showed: Hummus with Pita · Lemon-Garlic Fries · Tzatziki with Pita · Baklava · Mini Cupcakes.
Daytime (from history + app cuisines): **Classic Chicken Souvlaki Pita**, **BYO Mediterranean Bowl** (Lemon Rice, Chicken, Chickpeas, Tomato Salad, Tzatziki Sauce, No Quinoa…), gyro pita wrap, falafel, breakfast bowl, avocado toast, Aegean Sunrise toast.
Drinks: mobile orders may allow up to 3 → Proud Source + Pepsi + Diet Pepsi.
